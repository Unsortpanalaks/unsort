Questions
FAQ
Where do the tours meet?
Meeting points vary per tour. Once your booking is confirmed I'll send you the exact meeting spot with Google Maps link and everything you need to know.
Is the food included in the price?
Yes — the Food Tour price includes all tastings along the route. For walking and bike tours, any optional food stops are at your own expense (I always point you to the best spots).
Can I book a private tour?
Absolutely. Private tours are available on request — great for couples, families, or corporate groups. Just mention it in your booking form.
What if it rains?
We're Danish — we dress for it and go anyway. I'll provide rain ponchos for bike tours and we adjust the route slightly when needed. Tours are cancelled only in extreme weather, and you'll be refunded in full.
Are the tours suitable for children?
Yes! The walking and food tours are family-friendly. The bike tour works with kids who can ride a bike confidently. Let me know ages when you book and I'll tailor accordingly.
