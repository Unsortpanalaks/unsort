Hidden Copenhagen Tour

Behind unmarked doors and down passages that look private, there is a second Copenhagen. This is the tour that goes there.

2.5 hoursDuration
390 DKKPer person
8 peopleGroup size
Pay laterOn the day

Copenhagen keeps its best parts out of sight. The city is built around courtyards — green, quiet, and almost invisible from the street — and most visitors spend a week here without walking into a single one.

This tour is built entirely from those places. Passages that look like private property but aren't, a garden behind a doorway you would never push open, workshops and studios still operating in buildings that have housed trades for two centuries, and the neighbourhood bars that never bothered with a sign.

It works because your guide lives here. Knowing which door is genuinely open to the public, and which courtyard the residents are relaxed about, is not something you can research from abroad. Expect to spend the walk saying you have passed that entrance three times already.

What's included

  • Access to courtyards and passages most visitors never find
  • 2.5 hours on foot, roughly 3 km
  • The neighbourhood history behind each stop
  • A local guide with genuine knowledge of what's open and what isn't
  • Small group, capped at eight

What to expect

Quiet streets, uneven cobblestones in places, and a lot of doorways. We keep our voices down in residential courtyards — people live there, and the access depends on visitors behaving well.

Meeting point

Central Copenhagen, within walking distance of Nørreport or Kongens Nytorv. The exact address and a map are sent by email once your booking is confirmed, along with your guide's phone number.

Dietary needs & accessibility

Vegetarian, pescatarian, gluten-free and most allergy requirements can be accommodated with advance notice — tell us when you book so venues can be briefed. Routes are flat and can be adapted for reduced mobility or pushchairs; let us know and your guide will adjust before the day.

Cancellation policy

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour starts. Nothing is charged when you book — you pay your guide on the day — so cancelling costs you nothing. If we have to cancel because of unsafe weather or illness, we will offer another date or simply cancel at no charge.

Common questions

Is this different from your standard walking tour?

Yes. The walking tour covers the city's main story and landmarks. This one deliberately avoids them and goes into courtyards, passages and residential pockets instead. Guests often do both.

Are the courtyards always accessible?

Most are, but a few depend on the day and on residents. Your guide always has alternatives, so the tour never runs short.

Is it suitable for a second visit to Copenhagen?

It is arguably the ideal tour for a return visit, since it assumes you have already seen the obvious sights and want something else.

Can we take photographs?

Yes, though we ask you to be discreet in residential courtyards and avoid photographing people's windows.
